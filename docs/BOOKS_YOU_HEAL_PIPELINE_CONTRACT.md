# Books and You Heal Workspace Pipeline Contract

## Purpose

Provide durable, visible state for the existing Books and You Heal workstreams
without taking ownership of drafting, production or publishing.

The control plane tracks references, versions, checks, approvals, blockers and
handoffs. Source files remain owned by their established workstreams.

## Common file rules

- Source files are immutable.
- An edit creates a new version linked to the previous version.
- Source overwrite and deletion are denied.
- Cross-workspace retrieval is denied.
- Public/external sharing is denied.
- Rights and privacy states are explicit.
- Publishing and scheduling remain disabled.

## Books gates

A book item cannot become ready for approval until continuity, factual review,
sources and rights checks are complete. Approval requires a recorded human
content approval. Publication remains unavailable in this control plane.

Tracked state includes chapter mapping, research, rewrite, continuity, factual
QA, style, references, layout and approval readiness. No manuscript prose is
generated by this component.

## You Heal gates

A You Heal item cannot become ready for approval until claims, brand tone,
rights, privacy and visual-quality checks are complete. Approval requires a
recorded human content approval. Publishing, scheduling and spend remain
unavailable.

Tracked state includes topic, script, science review, brand review, recording,
transcript, edit, clips, captions, thumbnails and approval readiness. No content
or media is generated by this component.

## Human burden reduction

The state machine identifies only the missing gate rather than asking Katie to
re-review the whole item. When an item is blocked, its handoff should name the
single missing check or approval and link to the latest version/source.

The remaining human steps are limited to genuine judgement:

- content approval;
- later, separate publish approval if publishing is enabled by the owning
  workstream.

Credentials, private client information and unpublished source content must
never be pasted into chat or committed to GitHub.

## Rollback

These contracts are additive and do not alter live files. If later integrated,
disable the pipeline hook and restore the prior workspace configuration. Keep
all version and approval records as audit evidence.
