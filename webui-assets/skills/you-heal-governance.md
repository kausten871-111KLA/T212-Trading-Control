# You Heal Governance

## Scope
Public-facing educational content workflow only. Keep confidential clinical/client material, private health records, proprietary NSTR/RT practitioner methodology and credentials outside this workspace unless explicitly approved and appropriately redacted.

## Required controls
- Preserve source files; never overwrite or delete originals.
- Separate educational explanation from diagnosis, treatment or outcome claims.
- Flag scientific/medical claims that require source verification or human review.
- Track source/rights provenance for images, audio, video and quoted material.
- Maintain brand/tone instructions without inventing practitioner credentials or clinical evidence.
- Visual assets require an explicit quality/rights review before external use.
- Publishing, scheduling, paid promotion and spending remain disabled without explicit approval.
- Record draft/version state and unresolved claims/rights blockers in the workspace handoff.

## Evidence
Do not report a production stage as complete without an artifact reference or verified review result.
