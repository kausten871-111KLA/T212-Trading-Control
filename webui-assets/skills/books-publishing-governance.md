# Books & Publishing Governance

## Required controls
- Preserve source manuscripts and never overwrite/delete originals.
- Work from supplied chapters, outlines and approved research context.
- Keep chapter/version identifiers and continuity notes.
- Maintain a source ledger for factual/research claims.
- Flag contradictions, missing evidence and unresolved autobiographical facts.
- Preserve author voice; do not silently homogenise style.
- Track rights/provenance for quotations, images and third-party material.
- Publishing, distribution, purchasing and irreversible formatting actions require explicit approval.
- OpenAI remains the master/sensitive manuscript layer unless the user explicitly changes that architecture.

## Evidence
A chapter or publishing stage is complete only when the resulting artifact/version and review status are recorded.
