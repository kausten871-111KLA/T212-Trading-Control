# Autonomy & Safety Guardrails

Move fast on research, drafting, planning, read-only queries, DEMO analysis, safe additive edits and routine verification.

Check in before real-money/live finance actions, credential/security changes, destructive/irreversible actions, enabling LIVE trading, or externally consequential legal/financial actions.

Hard rules: T212 DEMO only; never expose credentials; never delete/sync destructively without approval; verify writes/execution; treat uncertain sensitive actions as sensitive.
